# Starter panels by deliverable

These are starting points. Fit every panel to the specific deliverable, then run the checks in SKILL.md. Each panel has a seat from outside the author's side and a seat whose job is disconfirmation.

| Deliverable | Starter panel |
|---|---|
| Startup pitch deck | Early-stage VC, growth equity investor, investment banker, technologist, go-to-market lead, customer representative, skeptic |
| Business or strategy decision | PE investor, growth VC, operator or former CEO, domain buyer, technologist, strategic acquirer, skeptic (full briefs in `personas.md`) |
| Board or leadership memo | CFO, operating executive who must execute it, independent board director, head of the affected function, customer or frontline voice, skeptic |
| Product spec | Product leader, staff engineer, designer, support lead, target customer, data analyst, skeptic |
| Technical design | Principal engineer, site reliability engineer, security lead, engineer who will maintain it, finance or capacity owner, skeptic |
| Marketing or brand launch | Brand strategist, performance marketer, sales lead, target customer, legal or claims reviewer, competitor's marketer |
| Sales proposal or RFP response | Economic buyer, procurement lead, end user, buyer's technical evaluator, incumbent competitor, delivery lead |
| Policy or process change | Policy owner, frontline employee who must follow it, manager who must enforce it, legal or compliance lead, affected customer or member, skeptic |
| Research or analysis | Domain expert, statistician or methods reviewer, decision-maker who will use it, practitioner, skeptic |
| Grant or funding proposal | Program officer, peer reviewer in the field, finance reviewer, beneficiary representative, delivery partner, skeptic |

If the deliverable fits none of these, build the panel from four sources:

1. The people who decide.
2. The people who pay or give something up.
3. The people who must carry it out.
4. The people who live with the result.

Then add the expertise the deliverable depends on, and a skeptic.
